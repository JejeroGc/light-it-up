# Light It Up v0.3.0 Alpha 6

## Interaction
- Direct dragging now moves the complete multi-selection while preserving spacing.
- Alt + drag duplicates a fixture and immediately moves the copy.
- Cmd/Ctrl + D duplicates the complete current selection.
- Delete/Backspace removes the complete current selection.
- Group selection, Shift selection and Cmd/Ctrl selection remain compatible with direct drag.
- Group movement is clamped to the usable width of the destination bar.

## Viewport feedback
- Added a live drag guide showing destination bar, offset in metres and fixture count.
- Added a compact shortcut reference and drag status to the viewport overlay.
- The receiving bar remains highlighted throughout the drag.
- Orbit controls remain locked while moving fixtures.

## Stability
- Based directly on v0.2.0 Alpha 5.1 selection hotfix.
- Cue playback, groups, presets, programmer, timeline and project serialization were left structurally unchanged.
