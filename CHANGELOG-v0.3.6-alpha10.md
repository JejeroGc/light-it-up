# Light It Up v0.3.6 Alpha 10 — Cue FX & Transitions

- Transition editor integrated into the audio/timeline toolbar.
- Per-cue transition: Cut, Fade/Linear, Ease In, Ease Out, Ease In-Out.
- Per-cue transition FX: Strobe, Pulse, Chase, Flicker, Color Chase.
- Speed and amount controls per cue FX.
- Transition settings persist as optional cue fields, preserving compatibility with older .lightitup projects (PROJECT_FILE_VERSION remains 2).
- Landing recent-project overlay corrected so live recent cards no longer collide visually with the baked empty state.
